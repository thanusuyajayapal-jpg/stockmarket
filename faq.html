Courses & Mentorship

Do I need prior trading experience to join?
Not at all. We have specific tracks designed for absolute beginners. Our "Stock Market Fundamentals" course will teach you everything from setting up your brokerage account to executing your first trade safely.
How do the live trading sessions work?
Live sessions are hosted daily via our private Discord server and Zoom. You will share a screen with our lead mentors, watching them analyze pre-market conditions, execute trades, and manage risk in real-time.
Do I get 1-on-1 access to mentors?
Yes. Pro-tier members can schedule bi-weekly 30-minute 1-on-1 reviews with their assigned mentor to review trade journals, fix psychological roadblocks, and refine their edge.
What trading platforms do you teach on?
Our primary charting instruction is done on TradingView due to its universal accessibility. However, we also provide execution tutorials for ThinkOrSwim, Interactive Brokers, and MetaTrader 4/5.
How long does it take to complete the beginner course?
The core curriculum is self-paced. On average, students spending 5-7 hours a week complete the fundamental modules in about 4 weeks before moving to live paper trading.
Are the live sessions recorded if I miss them?
Absolutely. All daily live trading sessions, Q&As, and Sunday masterclasses are recorded in HD and uploaded to the video vault within 2 hours of completion.

Billing & Accounts

What is your refund policy?
We offer a strict, no-questions-asked 14-day money-back guarantee. If you decide the academy isn't for you within your first two weeks, simply email support for a full refund.
Can I upgrade or downgrade my membership later?
Yes, you can easily manage your subscription from the User Dashboard. Prorated charges will apply if you upgrade mid-billing cycle.
Which payment methods do you accept?
We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Discover), PayPal, and selected cryptocurrencies (USDT, USDC, BTC) via our secure checkout gateway.
Do you offer installment plans or financing?
Yes, for our lifetime and Pro-tier annual memberships, we offer flexible 3-month and 6-month payment plans through trusted third-party providers like Klarna and Affirm at checkout.
Can I pause my membership if I go on vacation?
Yes, active monthly and annual subscribers can pause their membership for up to 60 days per calendar year. Billing will automatically freeze during this period.
How do I cancel my subscription?
You can cancel anytime directly from your User Dashboard under 'Billing Settings'. There are no cancellation fees, and you will retain access until the end of your current paid billing cycle.

House Rules

Academy Guidelines

We maintain a strict, professional environment to ensure the safety and growth of all our members.

No "Pump & Dumps"

We ban users attempting to manipulate low-float stocks or promote unverified crypto tokens.

Share Your Rationale

If you post a trade setup in the chat, you must include your chart and logical reasoning.

Zero Tolerance for Toxicity

Trading is stressful enough. We enforce a strict ban on insults, arrogance, or aggressive behavior.
