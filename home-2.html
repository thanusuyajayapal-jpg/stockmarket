Not at all. We have a dedicated 40-hour foundational bootcamp designed specifically for absolute beginners. We teach you everything from what a stock is, to advanced options strategies.

You can start paper trading with $0. When you are ready to go live, we recommend starting with at least $500 to $1,000 for options, or utilizing prop-firm capital to trade with larger size safely.

Yes! Every single live trading session, Q&A, and mentorship call is recorded and instantly uploaded to our video vault. You can watch them at your own pace anytime.
